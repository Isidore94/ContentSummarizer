# Bank of Canada Powerless

<https://youtube.com/watch?v=5R1OqsMiIFs&si=pJnMwcey7GOtfMVY>

**TL;DR:** The Bank of Canada is struggling to control the economy amid rising fixed borrowing costs and external pressures from global events.

**Key points**
- The Bank of Canada held interest rates at 2.25%, acknowledging weakened economic control.
- Future economic growth is influenced more by U.S. trade policy and Middle East conflicts than by the bank's decisions.
- Inflation risks are high due to external factors, limiting interest rate adjustments.
- The bank's messaging suggests a willingness to raise rates if necessary, particularly if oil prices increase.
- The housing market's response to monetary policy appears muted due to ongoing affordability issues.

**Notable claims & takeaways**
- Canadian GDP growth is projected at 0.7%, with more gradual increases expected in the coming years.
- The governor emphasized that inflation and economic conditions are not solely determined by domestic policy.
- Current indicators show Canadians are not heavily borrowing to cope with price increases, which could change with prolonged global unrest.
