# "How Should I Pay My Salespeople?"

<https://youtube.com/watch?v=G93HZdUlqp0&si=iQrRP4t6KBD0VNkO>

**TL;DR:** Derek from Lifetime Roofing struggles with hiring qualified sales reps and refining his pay structure, while exploring group interviews as a solution.

**Key points**
- Derek's company generates $7 million in sales and anticipates growth.
- Challenges include attracting qualified sales applicants and retaining them post-training.
- Currently, sales reps receive a four-week paid training, which Derek finds ineffective.
- Leads come predominantly from outbound calls (70%) and some door-to-door (30-40%).
- The interviewer suggests implementing group interviews to streamline the hiring process.

**Notable claims & takeaways**
- Suggests that a group interview format can help assess multiple candidates efficiently and identify the most suitable ones.
- Emphasizes that simplifying the hiring process can significantly increase business growth, citing a previous success story of a therapy clinic.
